Here's what I've found in my work with DTC brands: customers don't abandon carts because your product is bad—they abandon because they perceive risk. A vague, defensive, or buried return policy amplifies that risk perception at the exact moment they're committing.

1. Volume from recovered conversions outweighs incremental returns
