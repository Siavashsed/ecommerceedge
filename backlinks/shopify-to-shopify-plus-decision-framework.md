## QUORA
**Answer this question:** When should an ecommerce brand upgrade from Shopify to Shopify Plus?

Short answer: upgrade when the platform is costing you more than Plus charges, which is roughly $2,300 a month, and not before. Revenue on its own doesn't make the decision, even though most advice online treats it as the trigger.

I work with DTC brands on scaling, and the most common mistake I see is upgrading because a competitor did, or because the store crossed some revenue milestone. One founder I worked with was doing about $1.1M a year, had a 1.3% conversion rate, and signed a three-year Plus contract without having touched her checkout. Plus did nothing for her because her problems were somewhere else.

Here's how I'd evaluate it. Put a monthly dollar figure on three things:

1. Checkout losses. Plus lets you customize checkout in ways lower plans don't. If you have a clear, testable reason that's costing you orders (missing payment options, no way to add logic or upsells, friction you can't remove), estimate the revenue lost.

2. Workaround spend. Add up the apps and developer hours you pay for only because your current plan can't do something natively. Some of that disappears on Plus.

3. Manual operations. Look at the hours your team spends on tasks that automation (Flow, for example) or features like multiple storefronts and B2B tools could handle.

If the three numbers combined clear the Plus fee, upgrading is a sound business decision. If they don't, you're paying more for the same store, and the money is better spent on conversion work, site speed, or acquisition.

Also check contract length before signing. A multi-year commitment turns a reversible choice into a sticky one.
