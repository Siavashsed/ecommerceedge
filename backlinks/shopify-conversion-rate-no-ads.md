## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Why is my Shopify store conversion rate stuck at 1-2% even though I'm spending money on ads?

This is one of the most common situations I see working with DTC brands, and the answer usually surprises people: your conversion rate isn't low because of your traffic quality. It's low because something fundamental about your store experience is broken.

I worked with a skincare brand out of Austin that was spending $40,000 monthly on Meta ads with a conversion rate hovering around 1.2%. Their first instinct was to increase ad spend, thinking audience quality was the issue. We did the opposite.

We paused all paid traffic for six weeks and rebuilt from the ground up. We analyzed where customers were dropping off, rewrote product pages, streamlined checkout, removed friction points, and tested every critical element. No new ads. No additional traffic sources. Just optimization.

Their conversion rate climbed to 3.8%.

Here's why this matters: a store converting at 3.8% needs roughly half the ad spend of a store converting at 1.2% to hit the same revenue. You're essentially throwing money at a leaky bucket.

The most common culprits killing conversion rates are unclear value propositions on product pages, confusing checkout flows, lack of trust signals, poor mobile experience, and slow page load times. These aren't ad problems—they're store problems.

Before increasing your marketing budget, I'd strongly recommend auditing your actual conversion funnel. You might find that optimizing the basics gives you far better ROI than paid traffic ever could.
