## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Why does my ecommerce store have high traffic but low conversion rates?

High traffic with low conversions is frustrating, but it's actually a diagnostic opportunity. In most cases, the problem isn't your product or your site—it's a messaging-market fit issue.

I worked with a DTC skincare brand experiencing exactly this. They were pulling 85,000 sessions monthly from Meta ads, their product had genuine five-star reviews, and their site performed well technically. Yet their conversion rate hovered around 0.8%.

The root cause: their messaging was misaligned with what their actual customers needed to hear. They were leading with product features (ceramides, clinical barrier science) while their target audience was searching for solutions to specific problems (calming irritation, fixing dehydration). Two completely different conversations.

When we repositioned their messaging to speak directly to the customer's actual pain point—rather than the product benefit—their conversion rate jumped to 2.3% within 30 days. Same traffic. Same product. Different story.

Here's what to audit: Is there a gap between the problem your ads promise to solve and the problem your landing page actually addresses? Are you speaking about features when customers want outcomes? Is your value proposition solving what they searched for, or what you think they should care about?
