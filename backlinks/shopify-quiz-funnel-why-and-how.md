## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Are product recommendation quizzes worth it for a Shopify store?

Short answer: yes, if you sell more than a handful of products and your shoppers aren't sure which one they need.

I'm an ecommerce consultant who works mostly with DTC brands. A pattern I see all the time is a store with 25 to 50 SKUs sending paid traffic to a collection page. The shopper lands on a grid, sees a dozen options that look alike, and does what most hesitant people do: leaves to think about it. Most never come back.

A quiz fixes two problems at once. First, it replaces choice with a recommendation. It asks the visitor about themselves instead of asking them to compare products, then gives one clear pick. Second, it captures an email before the results. Someone who doesn't buy today still goes into a follow-up flow with a personalized product attached.

In March 2024 I built a five-question quiz for a skincare client with 38 SKUs. We used Octane AI and finished it over one long weekend. It didn't take a developer or a big budget. It took discipline:

- Five questions or fewer. Every extra question costs you completions.
- Every answer must change the outcome. If a question doesn't affect the result, cut it.
- Lead with one product. A results page with eight "matches" puts the shopper right back on a collection page.
- Ask for the email before showing results, and say what they'll get for it.

Quizzes don't help much in two cases: stores with three or four products, and catalogs where buyers already know exactly what they want, like replacement parts. There, a clean product page wins.
