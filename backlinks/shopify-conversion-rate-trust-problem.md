## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Why is my Shopify store getting traffic but no sales?

This is one of the most common problems I see as an ecommerce consultant working with DTC brands, and the answer is usually not what store owners expect. In most cases it isn't a traffic problem. It's a trust problem.

When someone lands on your store from an ad or a social post, they don't know you. Before they hand over a credit card, they're quietly asking: is this a real business, will my order show up, and will I regret this? If your pages don't answer those questions fast, they leave, no matter how good the product is.

Here's where I'd look first:

1. Social proof on product pages. Real reviews, ideally with customer photos, placed close to the add-to-cart button. Photo reviews are far more convincing than star ratings alone because they're hard to fake.

2. Shipping and returns clarity. Put delivery times and your return policy where people are deciding, not buried in the footer.

3. Proof that humans run the brand. An honest About section, a real contact method, and consistent branding go a long way.

4. Page speed and mobile experience. A clunky checkout on a phone kills trust instantly.

5. Payment options people recognize, like Shop Pay, Apple Pay, and PayPal.

I worked with a candle brand getting around 38,000 sessions a month at 1.1% conversion. Adding 41 real photo reviews to just their top three product pages shifted results within days, with zero extra ad spend.
